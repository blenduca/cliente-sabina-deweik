The identity is **pop color-blocking**: saturated hues (verde, rosa, laranja, azul, amarelo) cycling section-by-section on preto/creme, condensed all-caps Bebas Neue headlines, Space Grotesk body/UI, and a DM Serif Display italic accent word as the one signature move. Flat by design — no gradients, no photography, no grain, full pills for buttons/badges, square color-block cards, amarelo the signature accent. Light mode only.
